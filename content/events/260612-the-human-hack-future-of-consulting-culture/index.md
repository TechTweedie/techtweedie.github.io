I'm thrilled to announce that I will be speaking at **Experts Live UK 2026**! This is one of my favourite community conferences, and I'm honoured to be part of an incredible lineup of speakers.

### My Session: The Human Hack: The Future of Consulting Culture

In our session, we'll tackle a subject I'm incredibly passionate about: the evolving culture of consulting in the age of AI. We'll move beyond the technical hype and have an honest conversation about the human skills that truly drive successful outcomes.

This session is for anyone who believes that the future of our industry depends on augmenting our human skills, not just our technical ones.

### Co-Speakers

I'm excited to be co-presenting with two brilliant minds in the industry:

- [Areti Iles](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aretiiles/)
- [Daniel Barber](https://www.linkedin.com/in/danielbarber/)

Together, we'll bring a multi-faceted view on what it takes to build a thriving consulting culture.

If you're passionate about technology and community, this is an event you don't want to miss.
