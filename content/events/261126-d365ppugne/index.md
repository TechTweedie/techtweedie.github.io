**🕕 Time:** 18:15–20:00 GMT, followed by drinks and socialising

I'm looking forward to welcoming everyone back to Newcastle this Thursday for another evening with the Dynamics 365 & Power Platform UK User Groups community!

This month we're exploring two questions that matter more than ever as AI changes the way we design and build business applications: **How do we move beyond chatbots to useful agents? And how do we make sure AI-assisted systems remain understandable, interoperable and safe to change?**

We've got two fantastic speakers joining us: **Dylan Hayes** and **Dan Byrne**. Expect practical examples, architectural thinking and plenty of opportunity to connect with the North East technology community.

Our Newcastle user group brings together makers, developers, consultants, architects and people just beginning their Power Platform journey. It's an opportunity to exchange experiences, challenge ideas and learn from one another in a relaxed setting.

You don't need to be an AI specialist to join us. Bring your curiosity, your questions and a willingness to get involved.

## Agenda

### 18:15 - Welcome, Networking and Introductions

Catch up with familiar faces, meet new people and settle in ahead of the sessions.

### 18:30 - From Chatbots to Agents: The Modern Copilot Studio

**Speaker:** Dylan Hayes

Copilot Studio has changed dramatically. What started as a tool for conversational bots is now a broader platform for agents that can use enterprise knowledge, structured data, tools, workflows, reusable skills, MCP servers and even computer use.

Dylan will explore what modern Copilot Studio can do, how it fits into the Microsoft AI ecosystem and how to choose the right capabilities for a particular problem.

Expect fun, practical demonstrations using different integration points, along with a discussion of cost, capabilities and governance. We'll also consider when an AI agent makes sense compared with other AI tools or a more deterministic approach.

### 19:15 - Break, Food and Networking

Grab some food, continue the conversation and catch up with the community.

### 19:30 - Beyond Code Generation: Building Systems That Can Evolve

**Speaker:** Dan Byrne

AI can accelerate software development, but faster code generation doesn't automatically create systems that are easy to understand, integrate or maintain.

Dan will explore three architectural foundations for AI-assisted development and operation:

- **Metadata as shared understanding:** Explicit models of data, actions and interfaces help people and AI discover capabilities and understand behaviour.
- **Connecting AI to existing systems and people:** Agents and human workflows need to work through shared capabilities, business rules and permissions.
- **Keeping change controlled and traceable:** Authorisation, human oversight, observability and traceability help us understand what happened, who initiated it and why.

Drawing on lessons from building **NXTStack**, Dan will share practical ideas relevant to Dynamics 365, Power Platform and wider business applications.

### 20:00 onwards - Close and Drinks Social

The presentations might finish, but the conversations don't have to. Stick around to meet other community members and carry on the discussion.
