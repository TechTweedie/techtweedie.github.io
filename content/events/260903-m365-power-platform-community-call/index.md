## Copilot, Microsoft 365 & Power Platform Community Call

**Date:** Thursday, 3 September 2026<br>
**Time:** 7:00 AM PT / 3:00 PM British Summer Time / 2:00 PM GMT<br>
**Location:** Online via Microsoft Teams<br>
**Join the call:** [Join on Microsoft Teams](https://aka.ms/community/m365-powerplat-call-join)<br>
**Add it to your calendar:** [Calendar invitation](https://aka.ms/community/m365-powerplat-call-invite)

The Copilot, Microsoft 365 & Power Platform community call is back with a practical agenda for people building, governing, and extending Microsoft's business technology stack.

We will cover the latest news and updates, then move into three sessions grounded in real delivery work: simplifying Power Automate with a small amount of code, document generation with a custom SharePoint Copilot agent, and supporting WebMCP from an SPFx extension.

## Agenda

### The latest news and updates

A focused round-up of the developments worth knowing across Copilot, Microsoft 365, and the Power Platform.

### Sometimes a Little Code Saves a Hundred-Step Flow

**Speaker:** [Ian Tweedie](https://www.linkedin.com/in/iantweedie/)

Power Automate is excellent for many scenarios, but flows can become long, fragile, and difficult to reason about as validation, security, transformations, and error handling accumulate. Ian will explore how a small amount of well-placed code, exposed through clean reusable custom connectors, can replace dozens of flow actions without turning makers into full-time developers.

### Custom Copilot Agent for Document Generation in SharePoint

**Speaker:** [Katrina Frolkina](https://www.linkedin.com/in/katrinafrolkina/)

Katrina will share a custom Copilot agent for document generation in SharePoint, showing a practical approach to bringing Copilot into an everyday business process.

### Supporting WebMCP within an SPFx extension

**Speaker:** [Elio Struyf](https://www.linkedin.com/in/estruyf/)

Elio will demonstrate how WebMCP can be supported within an SPFx extension, with useful context for developers extending modern SharePoint experiences.

## Why Attend?

- Get a concise community view of the latest platform news.
- Learn when a small amount of code can simplify the flows your team has to own.
- See practical work with SharePoint Copilot agents and WebMCP in SPFx.
- Join the conversation with Microsoft 365 and Power Platform peers from wherever you are.
