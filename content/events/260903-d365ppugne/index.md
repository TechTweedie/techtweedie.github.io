## Newcastle D365PPUG - September 2026

**📅 Date:** Thursday, 3 September 2026  
**🕕 Time:** 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM BST  
**📍 Location:** Bank House (Cubo), 12th Floor, Pilgrim St, Newcastle upon Tyne NE1 6QF  
**🎟️ Register Now:** [Meetup event page](https://go.iantweedie.biz/d365ppug260903-reg)

I'm incredibly excited to invite you to another strong evening with the Newcastle D365 & Power Platform community.

Our September meetup cuts through the usual AI hype to focus on practical, advanced implementation that is changing how we build and deliver applications. 

We have two superb speakers joining us. First, Eswar Prakash will guide us through the critical structural shift from basic chatbots to multi-agent architectures and agentic workflows. Then Francesco Musso will show us how to explode our Power Pages potential using AI-generated Single Page Applications (SPAs) and an orchestrated team of pre-trained agents.

If you are a builder, developer, low-code maker, or technical leader in the North East looking for honest insights, genuine community learning, and a chance to network over good food and drinks, this is a meetup you won't want to miss.

## Agenda

### 18:15 - Welcome, Networking, and Introductions

Arrive early, grab some food and drinks on the 12th Floor of Bank House, and connect with other members of the community before the sessions begin.

### 18:30 - From Chatbots to Agents

**Speaker:** [Eswar Prakash](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eswar-prakash-r/)

Most enterprise AI implementation has looked like a game of ping-pong—inputting a static prompt and getting a static response. But that manual approach doesn't scale. The next major wave is built on Agentic Workflows and Multi-Agent Systems. 

Eswar will present a session specifically designed to bridge the gap between high-level business strategy and technical architecture, showing how automated, orchestrated AI agents operate in the real world.

### 19:15 - Break, Food, and Networking

Take a rest, enjoy some classic community pizza and drinks, and chat about the first session with other attendees.

### 19:30 - Harder, better, faster, stronger: Power Pages 100x’d

**Speaker:** [Francesco Musso](https://www.linkedin.com/in/franco-musso-power-pages/)

Thought you knew Power Pages? The game has changed completely. Liquid and legacy web templates are becoming a thing of the past, and you no longer need to be a complex pro-coder to build highly advanced pages. 

Franco will walk us through AI-generated Single Page Applications—showing how to squeeze the absolute best outcomes from the Claude plugin, look under the hood to extend and customize Power Pages, and scale up your delivery using an orchestrated team of pre-trained agents.

### 20:00 onwards - Close and Drinks Social

Wrap up the evening with our standard social, winding down with more conversation and community connection.
