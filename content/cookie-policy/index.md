## Cookie Policy

**Last updated: 19 October 2026**

Tech Tweedie is operated by Tweed Technology Ltd. This policy explains cookies and similar technologies used by the site at [https://techtweedie.github.io](https://techtweedie.github.io).

### Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small pieces of information stored in your browser. Other technologies can also store information or allow services to recognise browser activity. Some site features or external services may use these technologies.

### Advertising and Google's certified consent management platform

Tech Tweedie is applying for Google AdSense. Site ownership has been verified, but advertising cannot be assumed to be approved or live.

The official Google AdSense script is included on the homepage and blog articles for site review and to deliver Google's **Privacy & messaging** consent solution. The owner must publish a **European regulations message** in the AdSense account for Google's certified CMP to appear. In applicable regions (including the UK, EEA and Switzerland), the Google message offers consent choices for advertising; availability depends on the account configuration, message publication, region and previous choices.

If Google approves advertising, Google and its advertising partners may process personal data and use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalise advertisements as permitted by the visitor's choices and applicable law. Google provides information about its use of data in its [Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Analytics and third-party content

Our former Silktide cookie banner and Google Tag Manager integration are **not loaded**. The site may contain other third-party resources such as GitHub-hosted discussions, embedded media, fonts and shared user-interface components. Opening or loading these resources may cause their providers to receive technical data or set their own cookies.

### Your choices and withdrawing consent

Where Google's certified European regulations message is published and eligible, it presents relevant advertising privacy choices. When a site has been approved for AdSense and carries the Google tag, Google normally adds a **Privacy and cookie settings** link that permits visitors to revisit their choices.

You may also use your browser's cookie controls. Blocking some browser storage or third-party content may affect optional website features.

You can contact [privacy@tweed.technology](mailto:privacy@tweed.technology) to ask about this policy or your data rights. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for further information.
