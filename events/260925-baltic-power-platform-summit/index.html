Join Ian Tweedie at the Baltic Power Platform Summit for a practical session on custom connectors, small-step pro-code, and simplifying Power Automate solutions.

Baltic Power Platform Summit 2026

📅 September 24–26, 2026
📍 Gdynia | Pomeranian Science and Technology Park
🎟️ Register: Baltic Summit 2026 Tickets 🗓️ Agenda: Sessions

I am speaking at the Baltic Power Platform Summit on a topic that should be familiar to many Power Platform makers: when a flow has become too big, too fragile, and too hard to support.

The session is about recognising that moment early and taking a small step beyond low code in a way that makes the overall solution easier to understand, easier to maintain, and easier to evolve.

💼 Getting to Gdynia

By Air

✈️ Gdańsk Airport (GDN) is the closest airport and is located approximately 30 km from Gdynia.

✈️ You can also fly to Warsaw (WAW) and continue by train to Gdynia. The journey takes approximately 3 hours.

🚕 Gdynia has Bolt, Uber, or FreeNow. All services support online payments.

🚲 If you’re a cycling enthusiast, check out our local public e-bike system: MEVO City Bikes

My Session

Untapped Power of Custom Connectors: Helping Makers Take Their First Steps Beyond Low Code

Power Automate is an incredibly effective low-code platform, and for many scenarios a flow is exactly the right place to solve the problem.

But as solutions grow, makers often find themselves maintaining flows with dozens or hundreds of steps just to handle validation, transformation, branching, and other small pieces of logic that would be much easier to manage in a focused integration layer.

This session explores how a small amount of pro-code can actually simplify a solution rather than add complexity. Instead of building fragile, long-running flow logic, I will show how custom connectors can move that logic into a smaller, reusable component that fits cleanly behind a maker-friendly experience.

This is not a coding workshop and it assumes no prior C# experience. The goal is practical and confidence-building: recognising when a flow has outgrown its comfort zone, understanding how little code is often enough, and seeing how a small step beyond low code can reduce maintenance over time.

What You Will Take Away

  • Recognise the warning signs that a flow has become too large or too brittle.
  • Understand how custom connectors can keep maker-facing solutions readable.
  • See how a small amount of focused C# can replace complex logic.
  • Gain confidence to take the first step beyond low code without leaving the Power Platform.

Join Me

If you are building with Power Automate and have ever reached the point where a flow feels harder to reason about than it should, this session is aimed at you.

The focus is practical, not theoretical. It is about patterns, judgement, and confidence for makers who want to simplify rather than over-engineer.
